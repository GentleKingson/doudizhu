RTX 5070 reusable VRPO + CentralCritic launcher (LocalServer / Linux systemd)

Run on LocalServer:
  cd /root/doudizhu
  .venv/bin/python experiments/localserver_5070/launch_training.py --training-minutes 120

Defaults:
  Resume logs/localserver-5070-2026-10-12/train/checkpoints/ckpt_576.pt.
  Train for 120 minutes; save every 30 minutes; retain the latest 10 checkpoints.
  Chunk 32, CPU threads 8, rollout 512; restore the full checkpoint training recipe.
  Restore online policy, EMA policy, CentralCritic and both optimizers.
  Each invocation creates logs/rtx5070-<timestamp>-<id>/; old run folders are preserved.
  Rolling retention applies only to the new run's train/checkpoints directory.

Custom example (use an actual checkpoint path):
  .venv/bin/python experiments/localserver_5070/launch_training.py \
    --resume-from /path/to/ckpt_576.pt \
    --training-minutes 240 --save-every-minutes 15 --keep-checkpoints 20

Preview without starting a service or creating a run directory:
  .venv/bin/python experiments/localserver_5070/launch_training.py --dry-run

The launcher prints its systemd unit, log directory, status and stop commands.
SSH can disconnect after launch. To stop and save, use systemctl stop <printed-unit>.
A file lock prevents two instances of this launcher from training simultaneously.
Other GPU applications do not honor that lock: ensure enough free GPU memory.

Duration starts at the iteration loop, after checkpoint/model initialization.
Training and periodic saves finish at iteration boundaries; a final save is always
requested on normal completion. Initialization/compilation, the last iteration and
saving may add wall time. systemd has a fallback limit of duration + 15 minutes,
then allows 180 seconds for graceful shutdown. A hang or forced kill cannot
promise a final checkpoint. The checkpoint's original total_iterations remains
in force (40000 for the supplied checkpoint).

Replay, RNG and environment state are absent from upstream checkpoints. Replay
refills on resume; the result is not bitwise identical to uninterrupted training.
Do not launch run.py directly without the environment settings in launch.json.
The old fixed 2026-10-12 07:00 cutoff is no longer used by either launcher or runner.
